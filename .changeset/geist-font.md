The brand sans font is now **Geist Sans**, shipped with the package (variable, weights 100–900, normal and italic, SIL OFL 1.1). `styles.css` includes its `@font-face` rules. Apps that use Tailwind import the new `@renonreis/triquetra/fonts.css` entry point next to `tokens.css` and `theme.css`.
