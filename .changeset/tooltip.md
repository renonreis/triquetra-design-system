Add `Tooltip`, a short hint on pointer hover and keyboard focus, built on Ark UI with `Tooltip.Root`, `Tooltip.Trigger` and `Tooltip.Content`. It opens on top by default, keeps the content hoverable (WCAG 1.4.13), renders the content in a portal, and leaves nothing in the DOM while closed. Every default can be overridden through Ark's props.
