Add `Alert`, an inline status message with `Alert.Root`, `Alert.Indicator`, `Alert.Content`, `Alert.Title` and `Alert.Description`. It takes the same five tones as `Badge` (`informative` by default), shows a built in icon per tone, and sets no live region role unless you pass `role="status"` or `role="alert"`.
