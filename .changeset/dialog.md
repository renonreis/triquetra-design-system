Add `Dialog`, built on Ark UI's `Dialog`: `Dialog.Root` (sizes `sm`, `md` and `lg`; lazily mounted), `Trigger`, `Content` (portal, backdrop and positioner), `Header`, `Title`, `Description`, `Body`, `Footer` and `CloseTrigger` (a labelled "×" icon button, or `asChild` around your own button). Also adds `size.container.*` tokens, exposed as the Tailwind `max-w-{xs,sm,md,lg,xl}` utilities.
